Anchor

One calm place for your family's week.

Whose house tonight, Wednesday's practice, Friday's math test, and who's driving — in one app that parents and kids actually use.

Built by a parent, tested by real families. Anchor started as one family's answer to the group-chat-and-fridge-calendar shuffle and grew into a shared home base for custody schedules, school, and sports — where kids aren't just names on a calendar, they're users too.

🏠

Whose house tonight

Custody nights at a glance, months ahead. Handles blended families where each kid has their own arrangement — and skips all of it for one-home households.

📚

School, handled

Tests, quizzes, and homework checklists. Kids can add their own — they usually know before you do.

📸

Smart Import

Screenshot the teacher's slide or the team app's calendar and Anchor turns it into real entries. No retyping.

🏆

Sports & progress

Practices, games, and lessons on the calendar — plus metrics like dash times and throwing velocity, with progress kids can see and celebrate.

🙋

Kid requests

Sleepover? Play date? Kids ask in the app, parents approve, and it lands on the calendar. No more "but I asked you last week!"

🚗

Rides, sorted

Drop-off and pick-up on any event — so everyone knows the coach is bringing them home on Wednesday.

🌟

Streaks & rewards

Daily exercises and checklists build streaks; finishing everything can earn a reward request. Push them to be great — gently.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

The whole family, in sync

Everyone with your family code sees the same truth on their own device. Kids get their own view — even on a shared iPad.

Your family's data is yours. Full stop.

No ads, no analytics, no selling data, no tracking. What you type in lives on your devices and in Apple's iCloud — it never touches our servers, because we don't have any. Only people with your family code can see your family.
