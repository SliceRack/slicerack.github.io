Privacy Notice

The straight story on your family's data — no legalese.

📋 What Anchor stores

Exactly what your family types in: kids' first names, custody nights, activities and rides, school work and tests, and sports notes and metrics. Nothing is collected behind your back — there are no hidden profiles, identifiers, or usage trackers.

☁️ Where it lives

On your family's devices and in Apple's iCloud, which syncs it between your family's phones and iPads. Your data never touches our servers — we don't have any. Apple's own security and encryption protect it in transit and at rest.

🔑 Who can see it

Only people with your family code. Treat the code like a house key: share it with your family, not the group chat. If a code is ever shared too widely, email us and we'll help you move your family to a fresh one.

🚫 What we don't do

No ads. No selling or sharing data. No third-party analytics. No tracking. Weather features use your approximate location on-device via Apple Weather and never store it.

🧒 Kids

Anchor is built for families, and kids use it with their parents' setup — a parent creates the family and adds each kid. The app stores only what the family enters about kids (a first name and their schedule items), collects nothing else about them, and shows them no ads of any kind.

🧪 This is a beta

You're testing an app built by a fellow parent, distributed through Apple's TestFlight. Bugs can happen — keep anything court-critical in your official records too. A formal privacy policy will accompany the public App Store release; this notice is the honest, current state of things.

💬 Questions or deletions

Email stephen@slicerack.com — including if you ever want your family's data wiped. Deleting is quick and complete: your family's records are removed from iCloud, and removing the app clears your devices.
